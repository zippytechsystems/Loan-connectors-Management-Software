Platform Philosophy & Compliance

Bringing Calm, Confident Transparency to Loan Intermediation

Zippy Enterprises was built to solve the fragmentation, lost paperwork, and commission disputes that plague the loan connector and DSA ecosystem.

Our Mission: Neutral Technology Infrastructure

Loan connectors, chartered accountants, and DSA agencies are the backbone of credit distribution across India, especially for small businesses, manufacturers, and families. However, traditional referral processes rely on scattered chat groups, insecure email threads containing unencrypted customer identity documents, and opaque payout timelines.

Veridian provides a single, unified operations workspace that treats all participants with respect: connectors retain verified ownership over their referred leads, lenders receive pre-validated application checklists, and financial managers maintain a mathematically auditable commission ledger with complete reconciliation records.

Core Engineering Principles

1. Decoupled Customer Identity & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

Customer phone numbers and PAN identifiers are segregated in an isolated vault using blind HMAC SHA-256 deduplication and AES-256-GCM encryption at rest. Audit logs never record raw PII, and customer details remain masked on ordinary screens.

2. Immutable Double-Entry Ledger Discipline

Commission entries cannot be quietly overwritten or deleted. When a clawback occurs or a lender adjusts a sanction amount, offsetting reversal records are linked to the original entry with a verifiable audit reason.

3. Strict Tenant Boundaries

Every query and API call enforces non-enumerating tenant authorization. A connector attempting to query another agency's lead receives a generic 404, preventing timing and existence probing attacks.

Clear Non-Lender Declaration: Veridian Connector OS is strictly a software product. Veridian is not an authorized financial institution, does not grant credit approvals, does not make loan sanction decisions, and does not charge interest or credit processing fees to applicants. All loan contracts and sanctions are executed solely by authorized institutional lenders registered with regulatory authorities.
