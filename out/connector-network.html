Transparent Capacity & Governance Tiers

Every connector organization is assigned an operational tier based on documentation quality, verification speed, and historical throughput.

★

Tier 1 Platinum

High-volume DSA organizations and fintech aggregators with dedicated documentation desks. Priority routing dispatch, 50+ concurrent active lead capacity, and direct institutional liaison access.

  • Consolidated weekly commission settlements

  • Standard algorithmic routing
