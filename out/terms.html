Platform Terms of Service & Intermediary Code

The operational agreement governing connector onboarding, lead referral protocol, commission calculations, and ethical compliance standards.

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Governing Jurisdiction: Hyderabad, Telangana, India

Fundamental Regulatory Disclaimer

Veridian Connector OS is a business management and technology software platform designed for loan connectors, financial consultants, and referral partners. Veridian Connector OS is not a bank, non-banking financial company (NBFC), or money lender. The platform does not underwrite loans, make credit decisions, or guarantee loan approval, interest rate terms, or disbursement timelines. All credit evaluations rest exclusively with RBI-licensed lending partners.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing, creating an account on, or interacting with Veridian Connector OS ("the Platform"), you agree to be legally bound by these Terms of Service ("Terms") and our Privacy Policy. If you represent a firm, agency, or corporate entity, you represent and warrant that you hold full legal authority to bind that entity to these Terms.

2. Connector Eligibility & Onboarding

To register as an active referral connector on the Platform, you must satisfy the following criteria:

  • Be at least 18 years of age and legally competent to enter into binding contracts under Indian law.
  • Possess a valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) issued by the Income Tax Department of India.
  • Maintain an active, verifiable business bank account in your name or your registered entity's name for statutory commission remittances.
  • Provide authentic identification and address documentation as required during verification review.
  • Operate in compliance with all relevant guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) concerning digital lending intermediaries and direct selling agents (DSAs).

3. Customer Consent & Referral Protocol

As a connector utilizing the Platform, you assume strict fiduciary responsibility regarding data integrity:

Mandatory Customer Consent

You must obtain explicit, verifiable affirmative consent from each loan applicant before entering their personal details, phone number, PAN, or KYC documents into the Platform. Entering unverified leads or third-party phone numbers without documented authorization is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate account revocation.

Zero Upfront Fee Policy

Connectors are strictly forbidden from demanding, charging, or collecting any advance cash, processing fee, file-charge, or "guaranteed approval" commission directly from loan applicants. Legitimate loan processing charges, if applicable, are deducted transparently by the lending institution upon disbursement.

Document Authenticity

You guarantee that all uploaded documents (ITRs, GST returns, balance sheets, bank statements) are genuine digital reproductions of legitimate client files without alteration, forgery, or tampering.

4. Lead Ownership & Deduplication Rules

The Platform enforces automated deduplication logic using cryptographically salted HMAC hashes:

  • First-Touch Protection: The connector who first submits a verified lead with valid customer consent maintains referral ownership for that lead across the active pipeline window.
  • Duplicate Detection: Submissions matching an existing active lead by customer phone hash or PAN hash will be flagged as duplicates and routed for administrative review.
  • Cool-Off & Re-attribution: Leads rejected or closed without disbursement may be eligible for fresh submission after a 90-day cooldown window, subject to operational policy.

5. Commission Calculation & Payout Terms

Commission payouts are calculated in accordance with the tenant's active commission matrix:

  • Accrual Basis: Commission entitlements accrue only upon verified institutional lender disbursement and generation of an authorized sanction/disbursement reference.
  • Configurable Rules: Payout percentages and fixed slab incentives vary by lender, loan product type, loan volume, and effective date ranges configured by administrators.
  • Statutory Tax Deductions: All commission disbursements are subject to mandatory Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194H of the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961, and applicable GST offsets.
  • Clawback & Reversals: If a loan facility is rescinded, cancelled, or flagged for fraudulent submission within the lender's clawback window, the corresponding commission will be reversed via an auditable debit entry against subsequent ledger cycles.

6. System Integrity & Acceptable Use

Users of the Platform agree never to:

  • Attempt to bypass authentication mechanisms, manipulate role privileges, or inject unauthorized SQL or scripts into API endpoints.
  • Extract, scrape, or harvest applicant records, lender rates, or competitor commission structures.
  • Share login credentials, session tokens, or API keys with unauthorized third parties.
  • Upload infected files, malware, or payloads into the document review vault.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable Indian law, Veridian Connector OS and its operators shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages resulting from lender credit rejection, lender disbursement delays, banking settlement network outages, or regulatory changes in digital lending policy.

8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These Terms shall be construed, interpreted, and governed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any dispute, difference, or controversy arising out of or in connection with the Platform shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent civil courts in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.
