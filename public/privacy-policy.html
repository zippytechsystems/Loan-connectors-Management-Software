Statutory Privacy Disclosure

Privacy Policy & DPDP Act 2023 Governance

How Veridian Connector OS protects personal data, enforces digital consent, isolates KYC records in AES-256 encrypted vaults, and upholds data subject rights in India.

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 | Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Compliance Baseline: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) & RBI IT Framework Guidelines

Executive Privacy Commitment

Veridian Connector OS operates strictly as an intermediary business software platform. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest personal customer data for advertising. All customer data entered into the platform is collected exclusively for specific loan referral processing authorized by the customer and routed solely to verified lending institutions.

1. Scope & Legal Framework

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, transmission, and disposal of digital personal data across the Veridian Connector OS software suite, including public portals, connector workspaces, and administrative API endpoints. We comply with the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines for digital lending intermediaries.

2. Categorization of Data Collected

We process distinct categories of information depending on user interactions:

  • Connector / Intermediary Information: Full legal name, business entity registration, GSTIN, PAN, bank account details for commission remittances (NEFT/RTGS), operational territory, mobile number, and verified email address.
  • Loan Applicant Profile Data: Full name, telephone number, email, address, pin code, employment status, gross annual turnover or salary, required loan product type, requested facility amount, and lender preferences.
  • KYC & Underwriting Documentation: Scanned copies of identity proofs (PAN card, Aadhaar / Voter ID), income statements (ITR V, GST returns, audited P&L), and bank statements uploaded strictly for lender credit assessment.
  • System & Audit Telemetry: Timestamped IP addresses, session tokens, user-agent details, and immutable audit logs capturing every state change, document access, and commission calculation event.

3. Consent Management Architecture

Under Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, data processing must be grounded in free, informed, specific, unconditional, and unambiguous consent. Veridian Connector OS enforces:

Explicit Opt-In Timestamping

No loan lead record can be created without recording explicit consent affirmative action, capturing the date, exact time, client IP, and specific lender referral purpose.

Purpose Limitation

Applicant data is utilized solely for evaluation against institutional lending partner eligibility criteria and subsequent status tracking. It is never reused for unsolicited marketing or secondary cross-selling.

Withdrawal of Consent

Applicants retain the right to withdraw consent at any pipeline stage prior to formal lender loan disbursement. Upon withdrawal, referral processing halts immediately.

4. Technical Security & Encryption Standards

Veridian Connector OS incorporates defense-in-depth security engineered into every architectural tier:

  • AES-256-GCM Private Document Vault: KYC and sensitive financial documents are encrypted at rest with authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption. Documents are never stored in the public web root (`public_html`).
  • Zero Direct URL Access: Every file download requires role-authorized authentication and generates a short-lived cryptographically signed token (TTL ≤ 10 minutes).
  • Blind HMAC-SHA-256 Duplicate Detection: Customer phone numbers and PAN numbers are hashed using a private system salt to detect duplicates without storing or comparing plaintext values in indexes.
  • PII Masking by Default: Ordinary dashboard views, reports, and export files display masked identifiers (e.g., `+91 ******4421`, `ABCDE****F`) to prevent shoulder-surfing and unauthorized viewing.
  • TLS 1.3 in Transit: All HTTP communications require HTTPS with HSTS enabled, TLS 1.3 ciphers, and secure HttpOnly cookie flags.

5. Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC)

Access to personal data is restricted based on strict operational need:

Role Data Visibility & Permissions
Super Admin (`admin`) System configuration, user management, audit logs, backup execution. Raw customer PII remains masked unless an emergency unmask event is triggered with audit logging.
Operations (`operations`) Lead triage, assignment, lender dispatch, follow-up task management. Full pipeline visibility.
Connector / Agent (`connector`) Strict tenant isolation. Can view only their own referred leads and accrued commissions. No visibility into other connectors' pipelines.
Reviewer (`reviewer`) KYC document validation, checklist verification, compliance pre-checks. No authority to alter commission rules or issue payouts.
Finance (`finance`) Commission ledger calculation, disbursement approval, bank UTR recording, invoice reconciliation. No direct document modification access.

6. Data Retention & Disposal

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the primary referral purpose and comply with statutory financial obligations:

  • Active Applications: Retained during the referral evaluation, underwriting, and post-sanction verification lifecycle.
  • Disbursed Referral Records & Invoices: Retained for 7 (seven) years from the end of the financial year in compliance with the Income Tax Act, 1961, and GST accounting rules.
  • Rejected / Inactive Leads: Retained for a maximum of 180 days from the rejection date, after which uploaded KYC files are securely unlinked and purged from disk storage.

7. Rights of Data Principals (Applicants & Connectors)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, data principals hold enforceable rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Access Summary: Request an executive summary of personal data held, processing activities conducted, and identities of institutional lenders with whom data was shared.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: Rectify inaccurate, incomplete, or out-of-date personal information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of personal data where processing is no longer required for loan intermediation or statutory tax compliance.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit formal privacy complaints to our designated Data Protection Officer.
