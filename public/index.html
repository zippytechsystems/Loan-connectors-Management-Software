Core Capabilities

Built Exclusively for Loan Referrals & Connector Governance

Loan Connector Management Software eliminates manual spreadsheets, lost commissions, and untracked customer documents with end-to-end digital operations.

⇄

Deterministic Lead Routing

Multi-criteria dispatch engine evaluates applicant CIBIL, monthly income, requested volume, and territory boundaries to match with authorized institutional lenders.

₹

Transparent Commission Ledger

Accrue, approve, and track payouts with immutable audit trail. Distinguish available earnings from holdback reserves and reconcile NEFT settlement references.

🔒

Private Encrypted KYC Vault

KYC files and financial statements are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and stored in private vaults. Files are never exposed to public URLs or search crawlers.

📜

DPDP Act 2023 Consent

Digital consent receipts capture customer purpose, channel, and timestamp before lead processing. Blind HMAC hashing ensures strict PII privacy.

⏱

SLA & Follow-Up Reminders

Configure turnaround thresholds per lender and assign follow-up tasks to prevent applicant drop-off during documentation collection and verification stages.

👥

Tenant-Isolated Multi-Party Roles

Enforce strict role boundaries across Super Admin, Operations, Connector Agents, Reviewers, and Finance officers. Connectors only see their own pipeline.
